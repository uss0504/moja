===Natlan===
Unlike previous nations, most Natlanese Local Legends each have two additional achievements for fulfilling certain additional requirements; these achievements have to be done successively with each new spawn &mdash; it is not possible to get two achievements in a single encounter. Their achievements are found under the ''[[Duelist: Series I]]'', ''[[Duelist: Series II]]'', and ''[[Duelist: Series III]]'' achievement categories rather than the ''[[Wonders of the World]]'' category used by the previous Local Legends. The only exception to this feature is The Last Survivor of Tenochtzitoc.

All Local Legends, except Infinitesimal, are immediately accessible to the player.

;Night's Trials
These Local Legends are accessed through [[Nightsoul Totem]]s to take part in challenge fights called Night's Trials. Upon teleporting to {{LL|"Atlatl's Blessing"|,}} the player is forced to use their [[Traveler]]. Other Night's Trials retain the team the player was using.
